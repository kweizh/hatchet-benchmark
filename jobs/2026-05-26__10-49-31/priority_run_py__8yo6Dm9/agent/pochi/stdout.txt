## How priority reordering works

Because `slots=1`, only one run executes at a time. While the first priority-1 run is executing, the remaining 4 runs sit in the queue. When the two priority-3 runs are enqueued they jump ahead of the two waiting priority-1 runs. The resulting log (lines 2–5) will be:
```
3 <epoch_ms>
3 <epoch_ms>
1 <epoch_ms>
1 <epoch_ms>
```
satisfying the acceptance criterion that every priority-3 line precedes every priority-1 line among runs 2–5.
